
ReliefLink - Post-Disaster Resource Portal

1. Project Title
ReliefLink - Post-Disaster Resource Portal

2. Problem Statement
During floods, earthquakes and other disasters, affected people
often struggle to find food, drinking water, medicines, clothing
and safe shelters.

ReliefLink provides a common platform where people can view
available resources, find distribution centres, request help
and offer donations or volunteer services.

3. Project Objectives
- Provide information about available relief resources.
- Display distribution centre locations.
- Allow people to view the process of requesting help.
- Provide forms for resource requests.
- Encourage donations and volunteering.

4. Main Features
1. Home page with urgent needs.
2. Available resources table.
3. Distribution centre information.
4. Steps to get help.
5. Resource request form.
6. Donation and volunteer form.
7. Relief area map and image.
8. About and contact information.
